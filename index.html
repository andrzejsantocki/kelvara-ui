Two product layers

Observe without signing.
Arm a response by choice.

01Information layerRead-only

Understand what changed.

02Action layerRequires owner approval

Prepare the exit before an emergency.

After inspection, the position owner can simulate and pre-sign complete, exact-share withdrawal transactions backed by user-controlled durable nonces.

Simulate→Review exact terms→Wallet signs→Store encrypted
  • Restricted to the reviewed position, owner destination, instruction sequence, and fee cap
  • Unused variants can be revoked by advancing or reclaiming their nonce accounts
  • Mainnet fast close submission is currently initiated by the position owner
How armed evacuation works →

Clear boundary: read-only inspection never becomes signing implicitly. The action layer requires wallet authentication, exact transaction review, successful simulation, and explicit wallet signatures.
